**Subject: Help request clear answers about TPS for Ukraine**

Hi everyone,

We’re asking for your help getting a clear official answer about **what happens to TPS for Ukraine after October 19, 2026**. The attached letters ask members of Congress to obtain answers from the immigration agencies. You can send a request even if this issue does not affect you personally.

Temporary Protected Status, or TPS, provides people granted that status with temporary protection from deportation and permission to work in the United States. Ukraine received TPS because of the war. The previously announced extension runs through October 19, 2026. [Published extension](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/17/2025-00771/extension-of-the-designation-of-ukraine-for-temporary-protected-status).

**A decision was due in August.** By August 20, 2026, the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) was required to review conditions in Ukraine and decide whether the country still qualified for TPS. That was the legal deadline, 60 days before the current period ends. The law also requires timely publication of the decision. [TPS law](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/8/1254a).

**That deadline has passed, yet USCIS is saying TPS is set to end without explaining the decision.** The September notice from U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) says TPS and related benefits are set to end on October 19, but does not identify the formal decision or when it was made. It also does not explain whether protection or permission to work will continue afterward. For families who fled the war, this means uncertainty about their safety, jobs, and income. [September USCIS notice](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCISEVERIFY/bulletins/42855b0).

For people who rely on TPS, its end could mean losing protection from deportation, permission to work, and household income. In Texas, it could also prevent renewal of a Limited Term driver's license. Job loss could also mean losing employer-provided health insurance. Those who are not eligible for financial help may be unable to afford replacement coverage at full price.

The letters ask four questions:

1. Has DHS decided that Ukraine no longer meets the conditions for TPS, including through an unpublished decision? What is the exact date, and was it adopted by August 20, 2026, the deadline 60 days before the current period ends?
2. If no such decision was made by that deadline, how is DHS applying the extension provided by law?
3. What decision and publication in the Federal Register, the government’s official record of notices, support USCIS’s statement that TPS is set to end on October 19?
4. Do TPS protection and related permission to work continue after October 19, for whom, and until when? Where they continue, what documents can people use to prove it?\br

We want a clear, written answer from DHS about TPS and permission to work after October 19, so affected people and their employers know what to expect.
