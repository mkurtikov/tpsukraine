Dear Representative [Last name],

I am a U.S. citizen residing in [City], [State] [ZIP code] in your congressional district. I ask your office to seek urgent answers from DHS and USCIS about Ukraine Temporary Protected Status (TPS) after October 19, 2026.

[As a matter of public policy, I support an 18-month extension of TPS for Ukraine and clear guidance that helps eligible beneficiaries maintain authorized employment while protection continues. People displaced by the war need safety and reliable information about their ability to remain and work in the United States.]

Please submit a congressional inquiry addressing the following questions:

1. Has DHS made a final determination that Ukraine no longer meets the conditions for TPS, including any unpublished determination? Please obtain the signed decision, its exact adoption date, the deciding official’s name and title, and publication details. Specifically, was it adopted on or before August 20, 2026, 60 days before the current period ends?

2. If no such determination was made by that deadline, how is DHS applying 8 U.S.C. § 1254a(b)(3)(C)? Please obtain the applicable extension dates. If DHS considers that provision inapplicable, please explain why, including any reliance on a late or unpublished decision.

3. What decision and Federal Register notice support the statement in USCIS’s September 3 E-Verify bulletin that Ukraine TPS is set to terminate on October 19? Please clarify compliance with the publication and effective-date requirements of § 1254a(b)(3)(B), and request corrected public guidance if needed.

4. Please confirm whether Ukraine TPS and related employment authorization continue after October 19, 2026. Please identify the beneficiaries covered and the applicable end dates. Where protection or work authorization continues, what documents may people use to prove it? Please include guidance on Employment Authorization Documents (EADs) in categories A12/C19, pending renewals, and Form I-9 and E-Verify.

These questions seek documented facts and the agency’s legal position; they do not assume that an unpublished decision necessarily defeats a statutory extension. Please request a substantive response before October 19 and share the agency’s answers and the action your office takes.
